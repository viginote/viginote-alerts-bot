About VigiNote ZA

VigiNote ZA collects public reports from South African government bodies, Parliament, the police service, courts, municipalities and newsrooms, ranks them by how much they affect people, and gives you a short, sourced daily briefing.

We publish a headline, a short summary and a link to the original publisher. We do not copy articles. Each story shows its source and how confident we are: official source, multiple outlets, or single source (unconfirmed).

A person reviews stories before they appear here. We never name suspects or publish unverified allegations about individuals. If you spot an error, tell us and we will correct it publicly.

Not legal, safety or financial advice. In an emergency call 10111 (police) or 112 (from a cell phone).
